## 🚨 INJURY PREVENTION

### Shoulder Recovery Protocol
1. **This Week**: Professional examination
2. **Daily**: Ice 20 min, 2x/day
3. **Exercises**: Band work, light strengthening
4. **Gradual Return**: 25% → 50% → 75% → 100%
5. **Red Flags**: Sharp pain, numbness, weakness

### General Injury Prevention
- **Dynamic Warmup**: 15 minutes minimum
- **Cool Down**: Stretching + foam rolling
- **Recovery Days**: 1-2 per week mandatory
- **Listen to Body**: Pain vs discomfort
- **Professional Help**: Don't delay treatment
