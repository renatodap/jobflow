# AI-Daily Methodology

This page explains the editorial standards, content selection criteria, and operational principles behind AI-Daily.

## Sources Policy

**No Quotes Rule:** I never use direct quotes. Every insight is synthesized and rewritten in my voice.

**≤25% Overlap Guardrail:** No single source contributes more than 25% of any post's content. This is enforced via n-gram and SimHash similarity checking.

**Citation Format:** Sources are listed at the end of each post as "Sources consulted" — a simple list of links without commentary.

## Content Scoring Criteria

Every potential topic is scored on four dimensions:

1. **Freshness (0-25):** Released or updated within 72 hours scores highest
2. **Replicability (0-25):** Can a reader actually do this in 10 minutes?
3. **Impact (0-25):** Will this save time, money, or mental overhead today?
4. **Content Gap (0-25):** Is everyone else missing or misrepresenting this?

**Threshold:** Combined score must exceed 70 to enter the content pipeline.

## Post Type Definitions

### T1: Framework
"Use X when A/B, avoid when C" — Decision frameworks with clear boundaries and a scenario walkthrough.

### T2: Recipe/Playbook
5–8 steps anyone can execute in under 10 minutes. No prerequisites beyond basic computer literacy.

### T3: Synthesis/Comparison
Three tools evaluated on three criteria (speed/quality/cost), ending with Keep/Ditch/When recommendations.

### T4: Field Notes
What a new release or update changes for normal users today. Zero vendor hype.

### T5: Mini-Validation (Weekly)
One small measured experiment (10-minute test run) that anchors credibility with real numbers.

## Weekly Mini-Validation Rules

Every Sunday, I run a 10-minute experiment:
- Test a specific claim from the week's coverage
- Measure actual time, cost, or output quality
- Report unfiltered results, even if disappointing
- Include exact commands/settings used

## Limitations & Disclaimers

**What I Don't Cover:**
- Breaking news (wait 24-48 hours for dust to settle)
- Speculation about unreleased products
- Investment advice or price predictions
- Academic papers without practical application
- Drama, controversies, or vendor fights

**Accuracy Note:** While I verify claims when possible, I cannot test every tool on every platform. Your results may vary based on OS, hardware, and specific use case.

**Affiliate Disclosure:** Some links may include referral codes. These don't affect editorial decisions — I recommend only what I'd use myself.

## Buffer & Approval System

Content is prepared 2-3 days in advance and sent for approval at 7:00 PM ET daily. If not approved by 10:30 PM, publication is held until the next day.
