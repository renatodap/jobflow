## Email Sending Strategy

### Timing
- Tuesday-Thursday, 10am-11am or 2pm-3pm local time
- Never Monday or Friday
- Avoid holidays and local events

### Sequence
1. Initial email
2. Follow-up after 3 days (if opened but no reply)
3. Final follow-up after 7 days
4. Add to nurture campaign (monthly value emails)

### Personalization Required
- [Name] - Owner/manager first name
- [Business Name] - Their actual business
- [Competitor] - Local competitor doing well
- [Similar Business] - Business in same industry
- [Mutual Connection] - Shared connection if applicable

### Subject Line A/B Tests
- Test questions vs. statements
- Test specific numbers vs. general benefits
- Test urgency vs. value proposition
- Track open rates per template

### CRM Tracking Fields
- Email sent date
- Template used
- Open status
- Reply status
- Meeting booked
- Close status
- Revenue generated
